TinkerProof

Parental control for Android tablets and phones.

What it does: it turns the tablet into your child's home screen. It shows only the apps you allow, blocks the way into system Settings, caps the maximum volume of music, video and games, and protects the configuration with a PIN. It collects no data: everything stays on the device.
