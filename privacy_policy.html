TinkerProof Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

SUMMARY: TinkerProof does not collect, store off-device, or share any personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. What TinkerProof is

TinkerProof is a parental-control app that turns a tablet or phone into your child's home screen: it shows only the apps you allow, keeps your child out of system Settings, and caps the maximum volume of music, video and games. Data controller: Sanvaleisa LLC — contact@tinkerproof.com.

2. Data we collect: NONE

  • The app has no servers, creates no accounts, and asks for no email or personal data of any kind.
  • All configuration (the PIN, stored as a cryptographic hash and never readable; the list of allowed apps; the volume cap; the schedule and time limit; and the day's usage-minutes counter) is stored only on the device.
  • Nothing is sent over the internet. If you uninstall the app, that configuration is deleted with it.

3. Accessibility service

TinkerProof uses Android's accessibility service ONLY for parental control: returning to the home screen any app that isn't on the list you defined (including system Settings, app stores, the manufacturer's settings, the uninstall-app screen and the screens of the device's original home screen), closing the notification shade, capping music, video and game volume at the maximum you defined (the device's ringtone, notifications and alarm are not touched), and enforcing the schedule and daily time limit you set (pausing playback when it's time to rest). Incoming calls, alarms, the keyboard, system dialogs, the file or photo pickers an allowed app opens to choose content, and Google sign-in inside an allowed app are excluded and never interrupted. The service is configured so it does not read screen content: it only looks at which app is in the foreground and the title of the window, and neither is stored nor transmitted.

4. Children

An adult installs, configures and manages the app; the child only uses the home screen with their apps. TinkerProof shows no ads and collects no data from children (or adults). It complies with COPPA and GDPR-K by design: with no data collection there is no data to protect, sell or share.

5. Payments

TinkerProof is free while it is in its testing phase. When it goes on sale it will unlock with a one-time purchase billed through Google Play — you pay once, there is no subscription and nothing renews. TinkerProof will never see or store payment data: Google Play processes it under its own privacy policy.

6. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: to warn you if protection turns off (you can deny it; the app keeps working).
  • Querying installed apps with a launchable icon: so you can choose which ones to allow. The broad QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES permission is not used.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version ships with the app update and is published on this same page.
